01 — The work Ongoing

A SaaS website

I develop one website that provides software as a service. There is a place to arrive, a way to get in, and a service people return to. I am not going to say what the product is, who it is for, or which problem it takes on. The offering is the website itself, sold as a service.

A website Something a person can open, read, and come back to.
An account A way in, so the service belongs to the person using it.
A service Software that stays available after the laptop is closed.
